# PART A — NATIONAL PENSION SYSTEM (NPS)

## A1. Tier I vs Tier II

| Feature | **Tier I (Pension Account)** | **Tier II (Investment Account)** | Source / as-of |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nature | Mandatory retirement account; non-withdrawable | Voluntary, liquid add-on | [NPS Trust](https://npstrust.org.in/eligibility), Sep 2026 |
| Eligibility | Indian citizen (resident/NRI/OCI), age **18–70** at entry ⚠️ see note | Requires an **active Tier I**; same age band | [ClearTax NPS](https://cleartax.in/s/nps-national-pension-scheme), 2026 |
| Minimum initial contribution | **₹500** | **₹1,000** (some sources say ₹250) ⚠️ | [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/nps-national-pension-scheme) / [Paisabazaar](https://www.paisabazaar.com/saving-schemes/national-pension-scheme-tier-2/) |
| Minimum per contribution thereafter | ₹500 | ₹250 | same |
| Minimum per year | **₹1,000** (else account frozen; ₹100 penalty + arrears to unfreeze) | **Nil** | [HDFC Pension](https://www.hdfcpension.com/blog/nps-minimum-contribution-rules-what-you-must-know-before-you-start/) |
| Maximum | No upper limit | No upper limit | same |
| Lock-in | Until **normal exit** — see A4. Now defined as *15 years of subscription or age 60, whichever is earlier* for All-Citizen Model | **None** — withdraw anytime | [PIB PRID 2206763](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2206763), 19 Dec 2025 |
| Tax deduction on contribution | Yes (see A3) | **No** — except the **Tier II Tax-Saver Scheme for Central Govt employees** (3-year lock-in, counts in the ₹1.5 L 80C/s.123 pool) | [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/nps-national-pension-scheme) ⚠️ verify TTS still open to state-govt staff |
| Taxation of gains | Deferred; exempt portion at exit | Gains **fully taxable** (no special treatment; treated as income) ⚠️ no explicit statutory head — long-standing ambiguity | [Paisabazaar](https://www.paisabazaar.com/saving-schemes/national-pension-scheme-tier-2/) |

⚠️ **Entry-age note:** PIB (19 Dec 2025) records that the maximum **entry age was raised from 70 to 85** and the maximum exit/continuation age from 75 to 85 under the PFRDA (Exits and Withdrawals under NPS) (Amendment) Regulations, 2025. Several commercial sites still show 18–70. Treat "18–70" as the safe display value and **18–85** as the amended regulation ⚠️ — confirm against the consolidated regulations PDF: [PFRDA Exit Regs 2015, last amended 16 Dec 2025](https://www.pfrda.org.in/documents/33652/184762/PFRDA+(Exits+and+Withdrawals+under+the+NPS)+Regulations,+2015+%5BLast+amended+on+16+December+2025%5D.pdf).

## A3. Tax treatment — old regime vs new regime, old vs new section numbers

**The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaces the Income-tax Act, 1961 with effect from 1 April 2026** (i.e., it governs tax year 2026‑27 / AY 2027‑28 onwards). Deductions moved to **Chapter VIII**; exemptions from s.10 moved to **s.11 read with Schedule II**.

| Benefit | Old section (ITA 1961) | **New section (ITA 2025)** | Limit | Old regime | New regime | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Own contribution, employee | **80CCD(1)** — 10% of salary (basic+DA) | s.**123** read with Schedule XV ⚠️ *mapping not independently confirmed* | Within the ₹1,50,000 aggregate ceiling (old 80CCE) | ✅ | ❌ | [Olambit](https://www.olambit.com/blog/section-80c-new-income-tax-act-2025/), [ClearTax 80CCD](https://cleartax.in/s/section-80ccd) |
| Own contribution, self-employed | **80CCD(1)** — 20% of gross total income | s.**123** ⚠️ | Within ₹1,50,000 | ✅ | ❌ | same |
| Extra NPS deduction | **80CCD(1B)** | s.**124(3)** | **₹50,000** (over and above ₹1.5 L) | ✅ | ❌ | [Shahi & Co — s.124](https://shahiandco.in/income-tax-act-2025/section-124.html), [BharatTax](https://bharattax.co/income-tax/act-2025/section/124) |
| **NPS Vatsalya** (minor's account) | **80CCD(1B)** (extended by Budget 2025) | s.**124(4)** | **₹50,000 — shared with s.124(3)**, not additional | ✅ | ❌ | same; [Business Standard, 1 Feb 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/personal-finance/budget-2025-extends-tax-benefits-to-nps-vatsalya-under-section-80ccd-1b-125020101041_1.html) |
| Employer contribution — **Central/State Govt employee** | **80CCD(2)** | s.**124(1)–(2)** | **14%** of salary (basic+DA) | ✅ | ✅ | [Shahi & Co](https://shahiandco.in/income-tax-act-2025/section-124.html) |
| Employer contribution — **other (private/corporate)** | **80CCD(2)** | s.**124(1)–(2)** | **10%** of salary | ✅ (10%) | ✅ (**14%** from FY 2025‑26, per Finance (No.2) Act 2024) | [BharatTax s.124](https://bharattax.co/income-tax/act-2025/section/124), [HDFC Pension](https://www.hdfcpension.com/blog/new-tax-regime/) |
| **Aggregate employer-contribution cap** across **EPF + NPS + approved superannuation** | proviso to **17(2)(vii)**; accretion under **17(2)(viia)** | ITA 2025 — carried forward, Schedule III ⚠️ | **₹7,50,000 p.a.**; excess (and the income accruing on it) taxed as a **perquisite** | ✅ | ✅ | [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/retirement-planning/story/tax-rules-for-employers-contribution-beyond-rs-750-lakh-towards-epf-nps-and-superannuation-fund-explained-407870-2023-12-01); [Patron Accounting, 2026](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/provident-fund-taxable-interest-rules-2026-high-value-pf-contributions) |
| Lump sum at exit | **10(12A)** | s.**11** + Schedule II ⚠️ | **60% of corpus exempt** | ✅ | ✅ | [1 Finance, 25 May 2026](https://1finance.co.in/retirement-planning/new-nps-withdrawal-rules-taxation/) |
| Partial withdrawal | **10(12B)** | s.**11** + Schedule II ⚠️ | 25% of *own* contributions exempt | ✅ | ✅ | same |
| NPS Vatsalya partial withdrawal | **10(12BA)** | s.**11** + Schedule II ⚠️ | 25% of contributions exempt | ✅ | ✅ | [Protean](https://www.proteantech.in/articles/nps-vatsalya-new-rules-scheme-benefits/) |
| Annuity purchase | exempt at purchase; **pension taxable** as salary/other income in year of receipt | unchanged in substance | — | ✅ | ✅ | [Protean](https://www.proteantech.in/articles/lump-sum-revolution-with-nps/) |

> **Anti-double-deduction:** s.124(3)/(4) deductions cannot be claimed on an amount already allowed under s.123. "Salary" for s.124 = **basic pay + dearness allowance only**.

> 🔴 **CRITICAL MISMATCH (as of Sep 2026):** PFRDA now permits up to **80% lump sum**, but the tax exemption is still capped at **60%** of the corpus. The 20% slice between 60% and 80% is **taxable at slab rates**. Confirmed by [Protean](https://www.proteantech.in/articles/lump-sum-revolution-with-nps/) and [1 Finance (25 May 2026)](https://1finance.co.in/retirement-planning/new-nps-withdrawal-rules-taxation/). **No Budget 2026 amendment raising the exemption to 80% was found.** ⚠️ Re-verify before shipping to users.

## A4. Exit, annuity and withdrawal rules

Governed by **PFRDA (Exits and Withdrawals under the NPS) (Amendment) Regulations, 2025**, notified **19 December 2025** ([PIB PRID 2206763](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2206763); [consolidated regulations, last amended 16 Dec 2025](https://www.pfrda.org.in/documents/33652/184762/PFRDA+(Exits+and+Withdrawals+under+the+NPS)+Regulations,+2015+%5BLast+amended+on+16+December+2025%5D.pdf)).

### Non-government sector (All-Citizen + Corporate)

| Event | Old rule (pre-19 Dec 2025) | **Current rule** |
|---|---|---|
| **Definition of normal exit** | Age 60 / superannuation | All-Citizen: **15 years of subscription OR age 60, whichever is earlier**. Corporate: retirement/superannuation (unchanged) |
| **Normal exit split** | ≤60% lump sum / ≥40% annuity | **≤80% lump sum / ≥20% annuity** |
| Corpus **≤ ₹8 lakh** | (threshold was ₹5 L) | **100% lump sum**, or SLW, or SUR, or 80/20 |
| Corpus **> ₹8 L to ≤ ₹12 L** | — | Up to **₹6 lakh** lump sum + balance via **SUR (min. 6 years)** or annuity |
| Corpus **> ₹12 lakh** | — | ≤80% lump sum, ≥20% annuity |
| **Premature exit split** | ≤20% lump sum / ≥80% annuity | **Unchanged: ≤20% / ≥80%** |
| Premature exit — small corpus | ≤ ₹2.5 lakh → 100% | **≤ ₹5 lakh → 100% lump sum** |
| **Minimum lock-in for premature exit** | 5 years | **Removed** |
| **Death** | 100% lump sum to nominee | 100% lump sum; **plus new SLW / SUR options**; annuity optional |
| **Max continuation / exit age** | 75 | **85** |
| Joined after age 60 | 3-year vesting | **Vesting removed**; ≤80% lump sum / ≥20% annuity |

### Government sector — **unchanged ratios**

| Event | Rule |
|---|---|
| Normal exit | ≤**60%** lump sum / ≥**40%** annuity |
| Premature exit | ≤20% lump sum / ≥80% annuity |
| Death | ≤20% lump sum / ≥80% annuity |
| Small-corpus thresholds | Raised in line with non-govt sector |

### Partial withdrawal

| Parameter | Rule |
|---|---|
| Minimum membership | **3 years** |
| Amount | **≤25% of the subscriber's own contributions** (excludes employer contributions and investment returns) |
| Frequency — before 60/superannuation | **Up to 4 times** (was 3), with **4-year gaps** |
| Frequency — after 60/superannuation | **Unlimited**, with 3-year gaps |
| Permitted reasons (current) | (1) Higher education of children; (2) marriage of children; (3) purchase/construction of **first** residential house; (4) **medical treatment / hospitalisation** of subscriber, spouse, children or **dependent parents** *(widened from a closed critical-illness list)*; (5) disability ≥75%; (6) **settling a financial obligation to a regulated lender against a lien on the account** *(new)* |
| **Removed** reasons | Skill development / re-skilling; setting up a start-up |
| **New: lien facility** | Regulated lenders may place a lien/charge on the NPS account up to **25% of own contributions** |
| Tax | Exempt to the extent of 25% of own contributions — old **10(12B)** |

**SLW** = Systematic Lumpsum Withdrawal; **SUR** = Systematic Unit Redemption — both allow phased payout instead of a single lump sum, up to age 85.

**NPS-Lite:** small-corpus normal-exit threshold raised from ₹1 lakh to **₹2 lakh**.

**UPS vs NPS at a glance**

| | **UPS** | **NPS (govt sector)** |
|---|---|---|
| Payout | **Defined benefit** — assured 50% of last-12-month average basic | **Defined contribution** — market-linked |
| Inflation protection | Yes (DR) | No |
| Govt contribution | 18.5% | 14% |
| Investment choice | Restricted (default pattern; two extra options added Dec 2025 ⚠️) | Active/Auto choice available |
| Lump sum | 60% of corpus + service-linked lump sum | ≤60% of corpus |
| Upside if markets do well | Capped (surplus accrues to pool) | Fully retained by subscriber |
| Reversible? | **No** — one-time, irrevocable | — |
